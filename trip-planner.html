💡 AI Travel Tips
🎟️ Save on Entry Tickets

Buy the Paris Museum Pass (€52/3 days) to access 50+ sites including Louvre, Orsay, and Versailles.

🚇 Getting Around

The Navigo Semaine (weekly pass) covers all metro, bus, and RER zones for ~€22. Much cheaper than individual tickets.

🍽️ Dining Tips

Lunch "formules" offer 2-3 courses for €15-20. Evening meals are expensive. Make reservations for popular restaurants.

📸 Best Photo Spots

Trocadéro Gardens (Eiffel Tower view), Montmartre at sunrise, Pont des Arts bridge, and Seine riverside walks.
