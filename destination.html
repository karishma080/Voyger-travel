📖 About Paris

Paris, the capital of France, is a major European city and a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Its 19th-century cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.

Beyond such landmarks as the Eiffel Tower and the 12th-century Gothic Notre-Dame Cathedral, the city is known for its café culture and fashion houses, the Musée du Louvre and the Pompidou Center.
